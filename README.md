## 📋 How to Use the App

You can manage your course data through the main dashboard. 

### Adding a Course

1. Open NPTEL-Tracker from your desktop.
2. Click the "Add Course" button.
3. Enter your course name and duration.
4. Save the entry to see it in your main list.

### Tracking Assignments

1. Select a course from your dashboard.
2. Click "Enter Assignment Marks."
3. Input your current scores for each submission.
4. The system calculates your eligibility status. It shows if you meet the minimum required marks automatically.

### Predicting Your Final Score

1. Open the "Prediction" tab after you finish your assignments.
2. Input your final exam marks when you receive them.
3. The software displays your final projected certificate category based on the combined total of your assignments and exam scores.
